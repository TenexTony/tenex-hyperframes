# 12 · Blog post to infographic

## What it makes

A 45-second widescreen video explaining a post with charts and diagrams.

## What to attach

A post URL or the full post text. Optional: logo and useful source images.

## The prompt

```text
Turn [your post URL or attached text filename] into a 45-second widescreen infographic video, 16:9, without voiceover.

Explain [the main topic] to [your audience]. Use [your brand colors], [accent color], and [your font or "a clean sans-serif"].

Read the post first. Choose three ideas that explain its main point. Keep its numbers, units, and any stated limits accurate. If the page cannot be read, use attached text or ask for it.

- 0–4 seconds: a short headline introducing the question or problem.
- 4–16 seconds: idea one as an animated diagram, process, or clearly labeled statistic.
- 16–28 seconds: idea two as a comparison or simple chart.
- 28–40 seconds: idea three showing how the pieces relate or what readers can do with them.
- 40–45 seconds: the main takeaway, [your display URL], and the logo or [your brand name].

If the post contains no numerical data, use labeled diagrams instead of invented statistics. Use one main visual per scene and short supporting labels. Let each completed visual stay still long enough to understand it.

Keep any source credits readable. Save where each idea and number came from in NOTES.md. Keep the call to action on the closing card.

Show the three ideas and scene plan, then the preview. After my approval, run the project checks and export an MP4.
```

## Tips

- Choose this for explaining the article; choose prompt 04 for announcing it.
- A process diagram works well when the article has steps but no data.
- Ask for fewer ideas if the first draft feels rushed.
