# 08 · Talking-head edit

## What it makes

A 30-second vertical phone video with captions and graphics.

## What to attach

A 30-second vertical phone video with clear speech. Optional: logo, music, and a transcript.

## The prompt

```text
Edit my attached 30-second vertical selfie video about [your topic] into a short for LinkedIn and YouTube Shorts, 9:16. Keep my recorded speech and its order. Keep me visible while I am speaking, with graphics supporting the point.

Use [your background color], [your brand colors], [accent color], and [your font or "a clean bold sans-serif"].

First transcribe the video. Captions must match what I actually say. Check names and important terms. If transcription is unavailable, ask for a transcript.

Build a storyboard around these approximate beats. Adjust graphic entrances to the actual words rather than forcing the recording into these times.

- Beat 1, about 0–3 seconds: hook. Bring in "[hook line]" at the top.
- Beat 2, about 3–10 seconds: main idea. Keep me visible in the lower half. Use the top half for a simple animated graphic showing [main idea].
- Beat 3, about 10–18 seconds: problem. Animate [what goes wrong], then return me to the main full-frame view.
- Beat 4, about 18–25 seconds: fix. Show a diagram or labeled example interface explaining [the fix].
- Beat 5, about 25–30 seconds: payoff. Show "[payoff line]", then the logo or [your brand name] and "[call to action]". If I am still speaking, overlay the closing details instead of covering or cutting me off.

Add short captions near the lower third, away from my face and platform controls. Highlight only important words in the accent color. Use a subtle zoom at a new beat if it helps. Keep my voice clear; add a quiet whoosh when a main graphic enters.

Before building, show me the storyboard and wait for my OK. Then build a preview. After I approve the preview, run the project checks and export an MP4.
```

## Tips

- Record a 30-second phone video first, then attach it.
- Use beat text that matches your recording; graphics cannot fix a different spoken script.
- Prompt 00 can approve the storyboard and draft render while you are away; account permissions may still pause the work.
