# 16 · App promo

## What it makes

A 20-second vertical app showcase with a visual style you choose.

## What to attach

Three or more app screenshots or a screen recording. Optional: logo, music, and a style reference.

## The prompt

```text
Make a 20-second vertical promo, 9:16, for [your app name], using the attached screenshots or screen recording.

Audience: [who it is for]
Main task: [what people use it to do]
Three real features to show: [features]
Call to action and display URL: [action and short URL]
Brand colors and font: [colors and font]
Feel: [a plain description, such as "playful collectible cards" or "quiet and precise"]
Reference: [attached filename or "none"]

- 0–3 seconds: a short hook about the task or benefit.
- 3–15 seconds: show three real features, about four seconds each. Use readable crops of the actual screenshots or matching parts of the screen recording. Add brief labels that explain what viewers see.
- 15–20 seconds: app name, logo if supplied, call to action, and display URL.

Match the movement to my style description. For a playful style, screenshots can arrive like cards. For a calm style, use gentle changes and clear space. Keep the app itself readable and avoid decorations that compete with it.

Do not invent screens, features, reviews, or user counts. If you need a shot I did not supply, flag it or use a simple factual text card. Do not animate a tap that suggests a feature the app does not have.

No voiceover is needed. Use supplied music if available; otherwise a silent draft is fine. Show me the preview. After my approval, run the project checks and export an MP4.
```

## Tips

- Describe the feel in everyday words; you do not need a named design style.
- Record the screen if viewers need to see a tap, search, or other action.
- Use assets from your own app rather than copying another app's characters or branding.
