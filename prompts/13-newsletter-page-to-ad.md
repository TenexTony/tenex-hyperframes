# 13 · Newsletter page to ad

## What it makes

A 20-second vertical ad explaining why someone should subscribe.

## What to attach

A newsletter signup URL. Optional: logo, issue screenshots, and music. Attach page text if the site cannot be read.

## The prompt

```text
Read [your newsletter signup URL] and make a 20-second vertical signup ad, 9:16.

Newsletter name: [name]
Audience: [who it helps]
Display URL: [short signup URL]
Brand style: [your colors and font]

Use benefits, topics, frequency, and pricing only if they are stated on the page or in my attached brief. Do not invent subscriber counts, endorsements, free gifts, or results.

- 0–3 seconds: a short hook about a real problem this audience has.
- 3–11 seconds: show two specific things the newsletter helps readers learn or do. Use attached issue screenshots if available; otherwise use simple text and graphics.
- 11–16 seconds: show the verified publishing frequency and one specific benefit stated on the signup page. If frequency is not stated, omit it.
- 16–20 seconds: newsletter name, "[your call to action]", display URL, and the supplied logo if available.

Keep type large and screenshots readable. Avoid fake inboxes or fabricated newsletter issues. Any button graphic is a visual cue, not a clickable button in the MP4.

No voiceover is needed. Use attached music or an available track I can use; otherwise make a silent draft. If the page is unreadable, use attached text or ask for it.

Show me the preview. After my approval, run the project checks and export an MP4.
```

## Tips

- Show what readers get, rather than saying the newsletter is “must-read.”
- Use a screenshot of a real issue with a clear, useful headline.
- Put the signup link in the caption or video description as well.
