# 05 · Testimonial card

## What it makes

A 12-second square video showing an approved customer quote.

## What to attach

A short approved quote, the customer's name and title, headshot, and logos. For practice, use the labeled placeholder option below.

## The prompt

```text
Make a 12-second square testimonial video, 1:1, using the attached approved quote and customer details.

Quote: [the exact approved quote, ideally 15–20 words]
Name: [customer name]
Title and company: [title and company]
Style: [your brand colors], [accent color], and [your font or "a clean sans-serif"].

- 0–2 seconds: draw a large opening quotation mark.
- 2–8 seconds: reveal the quote in short groups of words. Highlight [the key phrase]. Keep the full quote visible long enough to read.
- 8–10 seconds: bring in the headshot, name, and title. Keep the quote visible.
- 10–12 seconds: finish with the supplied company logo and my logo, without making the frame crowded.

Fade in the quote and photo; start and stop each movement smoothly. No shake effects. Do not rewrite the quote or invent an attribution. If the quote is too long, suggest an excerpt for approval before building.

For a practice-only draft, use "[CUSTOMER QUOTE GOES HERE]", "[CUSTOMER NAME]", and a plain silhouette. Label it "SAMPLE TESTIMONIAL" throughout. If a real headshot or logo is missing, show the supplied name or brand as text.

Show me the preview. After my approval, run the project checks and export an MP4.
```

## Tips

- Use a quote the customer has approved for this purpose.
- A short quote leaves time for the viewer to read it.
- Replace practice placeholders before using the video as a real testimonial.
