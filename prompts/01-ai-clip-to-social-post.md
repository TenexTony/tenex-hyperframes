# 01 · AI clip to social post

## What it makes

A 20-second vertical social post from an AI-generated clip or still image.

## What to attach

One clip or image. Optional: your logo and a music track you can use.

## The prompt

```text
Turn the attached [clip or image filename] into a 20-second vertical video, 9:16.

Use [your brand colors], [your background color], and [your font or "a clean bold sans-serif"]. Keep all text readable on a phone.

- 0–2 seconds: bring in the hook "[your hook]" over the image or clip with a quick slide into place.
- 2–15 seconds: write three short on-screen lines using this description: [what the viewer is seeing]. Show one line at a time. Do not add claims that the clip cannot support.
- If it is a video, preserve its important action. Do not stretch a short clip or speed it up just to fill time; extend the scene with a still or a deliberate repeat if needed.
- If it is a still, add a gentle zoom and sideways movement. Keep the subject intact.
- Add very light grain and slightly darker edges only if they improve the image.
- 15–20 seconds: show [your call to action] and [your short URL]. Use the attached logo, or [your brand name] as text if no logo is attached.

Keep the clip's speech or other important sound. Use the attached music quietly, or an available track I can use. If neither is available, keep the draft silent and note that. Use a quiet whoosh on headline entrances, without covering speech.

Show me the preview. After my approval, run the project checks and export an MP4.
```

## Tips

- A hook of six words or fewer is easier to read quickly.
- Describe the clip plainly so the AI does not invent what it shows.
- Leave space near the edges for social app buttons and captions.
